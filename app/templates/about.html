About Us

Your trusted partner for discovering Nepal.

Who We Are

Darshan Nepal Travel & Tours is a Nepal-based travel agency based in Urlabari, Koshi Province 56604. We offer domestic and international travel packages, pilgrimage tours, trekking, sightseeing, school and team trips, vehicle booking, air ticketing, guiding services and more.

Our Mission

To help travelers discover Nepal — its mountains, culture, sacred places and hidden valleys — with reliable planning, local knowledge and genuine care.

What We Offer

  • • Domestic & international travel packages
  • • Pilgrimage tours (Badri-Kedar, Char Dham, Pathibhara, Kalinchowk)
  • • Trekking, hiking & adventure
  • • School, team & family travel
  • • Vehicle booking & hire
  • • Air ticketing & online travel services
  • • Guiding & helicopter services (on request)
